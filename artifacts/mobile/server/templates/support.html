Frequently asked questions

  • How do I sign in? Open the app and sign in with your registered email address and password.
  • How do I earn points? Points are earned when you pay at participating partner merchants through the app.
  • How do I redeem points? Use RewLo Pay at checkout — the app recommends the best time to apply your points.
  • How do I change my club? Go to Profile → My Club to switch your selected sports club.

Delete your account

You can delete your account and all associated data directly in the app:

  1. Open the RewLo app
  2. Go to the Profile tab
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm

You can also request deletion by emailing support@rewlo.io from your registered email address.
